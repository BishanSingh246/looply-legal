Looply Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Looply ("Looply", "we", "us") is a mobile app where you create and play short interactive mini-games in a swipeable feed. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Looply Android app (package com.looplyai.looply) during our closed testing (alpha) phase and after.

Contact: privacy questions and data requests — geofriendlabs@gmail.com

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Looply team operates the app and its backend. During the alpha, Looply is distributed only to invited testers via Google Play closed testing.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the app, keep it safe, and improve it.

2.1 Account information

  • Email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in plain text).
  • Username / display name and optional profile photo (avatar).

2.2 Content you create

  • Games you create (titles, prompts you type, generated game content) and drafts.
  • Comments, reactions (likes/bookmarks), votes, predictions, and submissions.
  • Uploaded media you provide (e.g. an avatar image, or audio for voice-based games). User-uploaded media is stored privately and, where applicable, held for moderation before it can appear to others.

2.3 Gameplay and activity

  • Scores, streaks, plays, completions, and play time.
  • Content affinity (which kinds of games you engage with) used to order your feed and recommendations.

2.4 Device and permission-based data (only when you use a feature that needs it)

Some game templates request device capabilities. These are requested on demand, used only while you play that game, and are not collected in the background:

  • Camera — photo/pose games.
  • Microphone — voice / loudness games.
  • Location — movement / distance games (foreground only; no background tracking).
  • Motion sensors — tilt-controlled games.

You can decline any of these permissions and still use the rest of the app.

2.5 Notifications

  • If you enable notifications, we store a push token for your device to send gameplay reminders, streak nudges, and re-engagement messages. You can turn these off in your device settings or in the app.

2.6 Analytics and diagnostics

We measure how the app is used so we can improve it. This includes events such as games played, time spent, feature usage, retention, and where people drop off (for example: template type, game id, play time, score, login method, screen views). Analytics is linked to your account identifier so we can understand per-user retention during the alpha.

2.7 Abuse-prevention signals

To protect the service (rate limiting and abuse detection) we may process a privacy-conscious hashed network/device signal. It is used only for safety and quota enforcement.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide core features: accounts, feed, creating/playing games, social features.
  • Personalise your feed and recommendations.
  • Send notifications you have opted into.
  • Keep the service safe: moderation, spam/abuse prevention, and enforcing limits.
  • Measure and improve the product through analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. EEA/UK)

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the app you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — analytics, security, and product improvement.
  • Consent — device permissions (camera/mic/location/motion) and push notifications; you can withdraw consent at any time via your device settings.

6. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Raw analytics/event data is retained for a limited period and then purged automatically.
  • When you delete your account (Section 8), your personal data is removed as described there.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise the export and deletion rights directly in the app (Section 8), or contact us using the email at the top of this policy.

8. Accessing, exporting, and deleting your data (in-app)

  • Export: you can request a copy of your data ("export account data").
  • Delete: in the app go to Profile → Settings → Danger zone → Delete account and confirm. This removes your login and personal data (profile, created games, comments, votes, submissions, uploaded media, and similar). Some records are anonymised rather than deleted (for example analytics and moderation audit entries) so they no longer identify you.

9. Children

Looply is not directed to children under the age required by your local law (for example, under 13 in the US or under 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), private storage for user uploads, authentication controls, and server-side access rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and respond to incidents.

11. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (for example where our service providers operate). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: geofriendlabs@gmail.com.
