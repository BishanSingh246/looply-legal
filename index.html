Looply Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Looply ("Looply", "we", "us") is an app where you create and play short interactive mini-games in a swipeable feed. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Looply Android app (package com.looplyai.looply) and the Looply web app during our closed testing (alpha) phase and after.

Contact: privacy questions and data requests — geofriendlabs@gmail.com

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Looply team operates the app and its backend. During the alpha, Looply is distributed only to invited testers, including via Google Play closed testing.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the app, keep it safe, and improve it.

2.1 Account and sign-in information

  • Email address and password when you create an email account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in plain text.
  • Google Sign-In data when you choose "Continue with Google": your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile photo. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or other Google content.
  • Handle / display name and optional profile photo (avatar). Your handle and display name are public; your full legal name is not.
  • Consent records, such as the policy version you accepted and when.

2.2 Onboarding and demographic information

After your first sign-in, we ask for a small amount of profile information. Some of it is required to use Create and Profile; some is optional.

  • Full name — required, stored privately, and used to personalise your account. It is not shown on your public profile unless you choose to use it as your display name.
  • Date of birth — required, stored privately, and used only to check that you meet the minimum age. We do not show it publicly.
  • Gender — optional. You can skip it or choose "Prefer not to say".
  • City, state, and country — optional and coarse. On Android we may ask for location permission to fill these in; if you decline, you can type them manually. We do not store precise GPS coordinates in your profile. Some game templates may request location while you play a game that needs it.
  • Handle — required and public. We generate a neutral handle and let you edit it.
  • Teen Mode consent (13-17) — parent/guardian contact details, consent status, verification method, and timestamp. We collect only what is needed to verify and record parental consent.

2.3 Content you create

  • Games you create (titles, prompts you type, generated game content) and drafts.
  • Comments, reactions (likes/bookmarks), votes, predictions, and submissions.
  • Uploaded media you provide (for example an avatar image, or audio for voice-based games). User-uploaded media is stored privately and, where applicable, held for moderation before it can appear to others.

2.4 Gameplay, activity, and purchases

  • Scores, streaks, plays, completions, and play time.
  • Content affinity (which kinds of games you engage with) used to order your feed and recommendations.
  • Purchases and subscriptions (if enabled): the product purchased, transaction status, credits, and subscription state. Payment card details are handled by Google Play; we do not receive them.

2.5 Device and permission-based data (only when you use a feature that needs it)

Some features and game templates request device capabilities. These are requested on demand, used only while you use that feature or play that game, and are not collected in the background:

  • Location — onboarding city/state/country prefill (coarse only), and movement / distance games (foreground only; no background tracking).
  • Camera — photo/pose games.
  • Microphone — voice / loudness games.
  • Motion sensors — tilt-controlled games.

You can decline optional permissions and still use the rest of the app. If you decline onboarding location, you can enter your city and state manually.

2.6 Notifications

  • If you enable notifications, we store a push token for your device to send gameplay reminders, streak nudges, and re-engagement messages. You can turn these off in your device settings or in the app.

2.7 Analytics and diagnostics

We measure how the app is used so we can improve it. This includes events such as games played, time spent, feature usage, retention, and where people drop off (for example: template type, game id, play time, score, login method, screen views). Analytics is linked to your account identifier so we can understand per-user retention during the alpha. We do not send your name, email address, date of birth, gender, or the city/state you enter during onboarding as analytics event parameters, and we do not use them as advertising audiences.

2.8 Abuse-prevention signals

To protect the service (rate limiting and abuse detection) we may process a privacy-conscious hashed network/device signal. It is used only for safety and quota enforcement.

3. How we use your data

  • Create and authenticate your account, including email/password and Google Sign-In.
  • Check that you meet the minimum age and keep underage accounts off the service.
  • Provide core features: onboarding, profile, feed, creating/playing games, and social features.
  • Personalise your feed and recommendations.
  • Send notifications you have opted into.
  • Keep the service safe: moderation, spam/abuse prevention, and enforcing limits.
  • Measure and improve the product through analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. EEA/UK and India)

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the app you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — analytics, security, and product improvement.
  • Consent — optional gender and location data, device permissions (camera/mic/location/motion), Google Sign-In, and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or via your device settings.
  • Legal obligation — age checks, child safety, and responding to lawful requests.

5. Google Sign-In and Google API data

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google authenticates you and shares basic profile data with us: your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile photo. We use it to create and secure your Looply account, show your avatar, and prefill onboarding. We do not sell this data, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers listed below.

Looply's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention

  • Account data, profile data, and onboarding demographics are kept while your account exists.
  • Parent/guardian consent records for Teen Mode are kept while the teen account exists and for a limited period afterward where required for legal compliance.
  • If you sign up and do not meet the minimum age, we delete the just-created auth account, profile, and demographics instead of keeping them. If a 13-17 signup does not complete parental consent within the allowed window, we delete the account and associated data.
  • Raw analytics/event data is retained for a limited period and then purged automatically.
  • Backups may retain copies for a limited retention window before they rotate off.
  • When you delete your account (Section 9), your personal data is removed as described there.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) also gives you rights to access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination. You can exercise the export and deletion rights directly in the app (Section 9), or contact us using the email at the top of this policy.

9. Accessing, exporting, and deleting your data (in-app)

  • Export: you can request a copy of your data ("export account data"). The export includes your profile, onboarding demographics, and Teen Mode consent records.
  • Delete: in the app go to Profile → Settings → Danger zone → Delete account and confirm. This removes your login and personal data (profile, demographics, created games, comments, votes, submissions, uploaded media, and similar). Some records are anonymised rather than deleted (for example analytics and moderation audit entries) so they no longer identify you. For a 13-17 account, a parent or guardian can use the same deletion path or contact us.
  • Deleting your Looply account does not delete your Google account. Manage your Google account separately at myaccount.google.com.

10. Children, teens, and age

Looply is for users 13 and older. Anyone under 13 may not create an account. Users aged 13-17 may use Looply only through Teen Mode with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. Teen Mode may be rolled out gradually; if it is not available in your region, you must be 18 or older.

For teen accounts we collect date of birth, parent/guardian contact and consent records, and the minimum account/profile data needed. We do not use teen data for behavioural tracking or targeted advertising. Teen profiles are private by default; public posting and comments are restricted; parental supervision tools may be available.

If you are in India, the DPDP Act treats individuals under 18 as children. We obtain verifiable parental consent through an authorised identity/age service or Digital Locker provider where required. If consent is not completed, we delete the account and associated data after a short retention window. If you believe a child under 13, or a teen without consent, provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), private storage for user uploads, authentication controls, and server-side access rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and respond to incidents.

12. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (for example where our service providers operate). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice.

14. Contact and grievance

Questions, data requests, or grievances: geofriendlabs@gmail.com. We aim to respond within the time required by applicable law.
