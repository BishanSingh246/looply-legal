Looply — Delete your account

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · App: Looply (Android and web)

This page explains how to request deletion of your Looply account and associated data. It is provided for Google Play and for Looply users.

Fastest way (in the app): open Looply → Profile → Settings (gear) → Danger zone → Delete account → confirm twice.

Steps to delete your account in the Looply app

  1. Open the Looply app on your Android device.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Open Settings (gear icon).
  5. Scroll to Danger zone.
  6. Tap Delete account and confirm when prompted.

Request deletion by email (if you cannot use the app)

Email geofriendlabs@gmail.com from the same email address associated with your Looply account. Subject line example: Looply account deletion request. Include your Looply handle or account email. We will process the request and confirm when complete.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove your login and personal data associated with that account, including for example:

  • Profile (handle, display name, avatar)
  • Onboarding demographics (full name, date of birth, gender, city/state/country)
  • Teen Mode profile data and parent/guardian consent link (the consent record may be kept for legal compliance)
  • Games you created
  • Comments, reactions, votes, predictions, and submissions tied to your account
  • Uploaded media stored for your account (e.g. avatar / private UGC where applicable)
  • Push tokens for your devices

Teen accounts (13-17)

A parent or legal guardian can request deletion of a teen account using the same in-app path or the email request below. If a 13-17 signup does not complete the closed-beta parent acknowledgement or verifiable parental consent within the allowed window, we delete the account and associated data automatically.

If you signed in with Google

Deleting your Looply account does not delete your Google account or other Google services. To manage or delete your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com.

What data may be kept or anonymised

  • Some analytics and moderation/audit records may be anonymised (personal identifiers removed) rather than fully erased, so they no longer identify you.
  • Parent/guardian consent records for Teen Mode may be retained for a limited period where required for legal compliance.
  • Backups may retain copies for a limited retention window before they rotate off.

Partial data deletion without deleting the account

Looply does not currently offer a separate “delete some data but keep the account” flow beyond editing/removing content you created (e.g. deleting a game or comment where the product allows). To remove your personal data comprehensively, use full account deletion above. You can also email geofriendlabs@gmail.com for help.
